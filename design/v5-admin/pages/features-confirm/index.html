Turn off Share cards and link previews?

This is visible to players. Turning it off also affects:

Link previews — Discord, Reddit and X show the plain Everstats preview
Collections share card — hidden (depends on Share cards)
Share buttons — removed from character pages, guild pages and board rows
Cached cards — kept for 7 days in case you turn it back on
