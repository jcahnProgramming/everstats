# Card maker — from a board row

Any visitor: leaderboard card, story, Top 3 context, period picker. The viewer isn’t the owner, so they get the "choices aren’t saved" note.

Built on v1 (top bar, footer, `.panel`, `.seg`, `.toggle`, `.btn`, `.note`, `.toast`) and v2 (player inline badge in the header). The preview images are the example renders in `../../examples/` and follow the selected theme. Open `index.html?theme=alliance|horde`. Screenshots: `screenshots/{desktop,phone}-{theme}.png`.
